Privacy Policy

Applies to all Okkodex apps on Google Play · Last updated November 20, 2026

Okkodex ("we") builds apps that keep your information on your device. This policy explains what that means for each app we publish on Google Play.

Information we collect

None. Our apps do not ask for an account, do not collect personal information, and do not include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Your progress, settings and history are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Information we share

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Network use

  • Android TV Remote connects only to the TVs you choose on your own local Wi‑Fi network, to send remote-control commands. It does not send your information to us or to anyone else.
  • IC Dictionary may open a manufacturer's datasheet when you ask it to. That request goes directly to the manufacturer's website.
  • Our other apps work fully offline.

Children

Our apps are rated for everyone and collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
