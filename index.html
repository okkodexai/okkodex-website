About Okkodex

Software that earns its place on your phone.


Okkodex is a small, independent software studio. We make apps we'd want to use ourselves: focused, fast and honest about what they do.

Most of our work sits where learning meets doing. You can simulate a circuit before you solder it, decode a chip on the workbench, or master the periodic table one short round at a time.

We build every app to be accessible, with dark themes, screen-reader labels, colour-blind modes and adjustable text, because good tools should work for everyone.
